  • SAGARIS is an agentic revenue OS: a single system that unifies CRM, dialers, inbox, SMS, an AI receptionist, coaching, and revenue analytics, where AI agents act on your data instead of just storing it.

  • SAGARIS is built by SAGARIS Inc, a software company founded in 2024 and working hybrid out of McLean, Virginia, in the Washington DC area. The company builds one product: the AI sales platform described on this page. If you searched for the TVR Sagaris sports car or the Scythian battle-axe it was named after, this is a different Sagaris. It was founded by Suleman Siddiqui.

  • A CRM records what your team did. SAGARIS acts: agents log activity, draft follow-ups, advance stages on real signals and surface the next move, so the system does the work a CRM only files away. The practical difference is that nobody has to update the record afterwards, because the work and the record are the same action.

  • One seat replaces the typical stack of CRM, dialer, sequencer, inbox, data enrichment, and conversation intelligence tools: a $1,200-plus per-rep spread collapsed into a single line item. If the dialer is the first line you are replacing, our Nooks alternatives page reads that choice at source, with dates.

  • Yes. SAGARIS reads Salesforce and HubSpot live and connects to the mail, calendar and telephony you already run, so it augments your system of record rather than forcing a rip-and-replace. Write-back to either CRM is built and switched off in production, so it runs as a dry run: SAGARIS computes the change and records it without sending it.

  • Security and privacy are core to the platform: encrypted data handling, role-based access, approval gates, and an audit trail of the AI agent's actions, data imports and exports. SOC 2 and ISO 27001 work is underway, with GDPR-ready controls today.

  • Most teams are live in days, not quarters. A revenue engineer walks you through the import, the CRM sync and the guardrails, and the engine starts logging and drafting from your real pipeline immediately rather than after a configuration project. There is no implementation phase to budget for before the first useful output.
